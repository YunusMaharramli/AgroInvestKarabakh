# Rental marketplace: legal requirements and field matrix

**Checked:** 2026-10-15. This is an implementation research note, not a legal opinion. The linked e-qanun.az texts are the authoritative source set consulted. A lawyer should verify the current consolidated text and apply it to each property and transaction before publication or contract execution.

## Legal requirements mapped to platform behavior

| Verified rule and source | Platform field, document, or workflow | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Land Code Arts. 48(2)–(3), 51(1)–(4): Azerbaijani citizens and legal entities may acquire land through ownership, use, or lease. Foreign individuals, stateless persons, foreign legal entities, and the listed foreign organizations/states may acquire land only through lease rights. A land lease is paid, temporary use under a contract; the lessor depends on public/private ownership. | Applicant types stay separate from `USER`/`ADMIN` authorization roles. Foreign applicant subtype is represented as an individual or legal entity. The listing workflow is for lease offers only; it does not offer land ownership. Each listing records owner category, authority basis, legal designation, lease terms, and procedure. | Verified legal rule; the exact eligibility of a transaction still depends on the property and special rules. |
| Land Code Arts. 4(3), 10, 12–13; Land Lease Law Art. 9: land status includes its designated purpose and restrictions. Agricultural land is specially protected, must be used for agricultural purposes, and its quality/fertility must be protected. | Public listing captures legal designation and permitted use. Admins must state use/eligibility terms. Applicants provide intended use. Admin review must compare the proposed use to the recorded status. | Verified legal rule. The app does not independently verify cadastral records or adjudicate permitted use. |
| Land Lease Law Art. 10: land can be leased by owner/authorized authority directly or through competitions/auctions, subject to exceptions. | Land Lease Law Art. 10-1: state-owned agricultural land has its own application and approval procedure. Art. 10-1.2 lists procedure-specific documents, including identity document for a natural person; registry extract for legal entities/foreign-entity branches; tax registration proof; economic justification; and, in applicable cases, land-user consent. Arts. 10-1.8–.9 add specific investment-competition and resident-preference rules for agricultural land in liberated territories. | Configure documents and external procedure on each affected listing. This only records an administrator's marketplace record. It does not file a registry application or prove registration. | Verified legal rule; execution and registration occur outside this application. |
| Land Lease Law Art. 11: land lease contracts include party identifiers/addresses, land size, quality/category, boundaries/designation, term, rent/payment conditions, land use/protection, changes/termination, and liability. | The listing and applicant data capture some informational fields; the app does not generate or sign a legally complete contract. ## Applicant profile and document matrix

The “required in form” entries below are application/business validation choices. They are **not** a declaration that legislation universally requires those fields. FIN, VÖEN, local residency, an Azerbaijani phone number, passport details, or an uploaded identity document are not universal platform requirements.

| Applicant type | Form fields required by this platform | Optional profile fields | Documents and legal status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual | Full legal name; contact phone; contact email; profile processing consent | Citizenship, country of residence, residence/contact address, ID type/number, FIN, representative data | No identity upload is globally required by the profile. A listing-specific rule or special statutory procedure may require supporting identity/representation evidence. |
| Legal entity | Registered legal name, legal form, country of incorporation, registration identifier, representative name/position, authority-to-act description; contact phone/email; profile processing consent | VÖEN/tax identifier, registered/contact address | No registration extract or mandate is globally required by the profile. Add it only for an applicable listing/procedure after legal review. |
| Foreign individual | Full legal name as shown on identity document, citizenship, country of residence; contact phone/email; profile processing consent | Residence address, ID type/number, local contact/residency details, representative data | No passport upload, translation, legalization, or apostille is configured as a universal rule. Any such requirement must be justified by the specific procedure and configured per listing. |
| Foreign legal entity | Registered legal name, legal form, country of incorporation, registration identifier, representative name/position, authority-to-act description; contact phone/email; profile processing consent | Registered/contact address, local tax identifier | No translated, legalized, or apostilled corporate document is globally required. Configure evidence only where the governing procedure requires it. |

Admin-configurable document scopes are `ALL`, `INDIVIDUAL`, `LEGAL_ENTITY`, `FOREIGN_INDIVIDUAL`, and `FOREIGN_LEGAL_ENTITY`. They describe platform rules for a particular listing; a rule configured by an administrator is not by itself a verified statutory requirement. Publishing requires at least one scoped document rule, and administrators must check the reason for each one. Files remain private behind authenticated document routes.

## Ownership, property class, and eligibility boundaries

- This product models paid temporary lease/use. Land Code Art. 48(3) distinguishes land ownership from leasing for foreign persons: the listed foreign categories are limited to land lease rights. Do not translate that statement into a guarantee that a particular foreign applicant is eligible for a particular listing.
- Private land direct leasing requires the notarized agreement described in Land Lease Law Art. 10. Municipal and state land procedures differ and can require a competition/auction, a competent public lessor, and a special statutory workflow. A private portal administrator cannot substitute for those authorities.
- State agricultural land has the distinct Art. 10-1 process. For land in liberated territories, Arts. 10-1.8–.9 specify investment competitions for business entities and a separate preferential path for certain residents; applicability and detailed rules should be checked with the responsible authority.
- Business properties may be state, municipal, or private immovable assets and can have additional rules. The cited state-property competition rules cover a defined group of assets and exclusions; do not treat them as a universal rule for every building.
- Listing approval, application approval, and a recorded lease in this software are platform states only. They do not create authority, award a tender, execute/notarize an agreement, register a right, or establish that the premises can legally be leased.

## Personal-data and operational decisions still needed

Before collecting real applicant documents in production, the controller should obtain legal review and implement at least the following:

1. Identify the legal data controller/operator in the consent and privacy notice; state purpose, specific data/operations, consent duration and withdrawal method, retention period, deletion/archive conditions, and applicable legal basis.
2. Decide and implement retention/deletion schedules for profile data, application snapshots, audit records, and uploaded files, accounting for legal record-keeping duties. Provide access/correction/objection/deletion request handling as required.
3. Determine whether the personal-data information system must be registered or qualifies for an exception. Review hosting location, subprocessors, cross-border access/transfer, and any third-party disclosure before enabling them.
4. Have counsel confirm document evidence and authority checks for each private, municipal, state, agricultural, liberated-territory, and business-property listing. Confirm current auction/tender/consent/notarization/registration steps and the competent lessor.
5. Define whether an admin may attest to authority, which documents establish that authority, and how identity, representation, sanctions/eligibility, and document expiry are checked. The current “authority reviewed” checkbox is an internal control, not a legal determination.

## Existing data and migration behavior

Rental tables are added through EF Core migrations. Existing `lands`, `proposals`, user accounts, and their records are not reclassified or deleted. The old land/proposal tables remain in the model/database, and regular-user owner-submission routes are removed from the active API/UI. Legacy records stay outside this rental workflow pending explicit review and an approved mapping.
